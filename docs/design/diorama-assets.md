# Diorama artwork and app assets

The complete source package lives in `artwork/diorama/source-package/`. It retains the supplied 89 full-resolution PNG crops, the 13 source atlases, the original prompts, atlas manifests, item sidecars, and crop rectangles. `artwork/diorama/manifest.json` records the app output and the SHA-256 of each crop, atlas, and prompt. The build checks each crop against its atlas rectangle pixel-for-pixel before exporting it.

`node tools/build-diorama-assets.cjs` reproduces app renditions from that preserved package. To initialize the archive from the original package, run `node tools/build-diorama-assets.cjs --source="<path to diorama-production-2026-10-24>"`. It writes WebP files under `app/src/main/assets/game/mobile-assets/diorama/` and the global/CommonJS `DioramaAssets` mapping in `app/src/main/assets/game/mobile-diorama-assets.js`.

App renditions preserve aspect ratio and never enlarge source art. Maximum dimensions are 512 px for buildings and equipment, 384 px for jobs, 256 px for resources and terrain, 448 px for biomes, 640 px for scenes, and 512 px for interface materials. Building, equipment, resource, UI-material (`U01`–`U06`), and terrain-tile (`T01`–`T03`) renditions trim transparent outer padding to the pixels with alpha above 8, keeping a two-pixel safety margin where the source allows it. The complete source silhouettes and disconnected visible pieces remain within the crop; source PNGs and original atlas crop coordinates are unchanged. This lets CSS scale each image across its available frame; the transparent opening in `U06` remains transparent. Encoding uses WebP quality 92 with quality 100 alpha.

Matte cleanup is deliberately limited: semi-transparent pixels with near-pure red (`R ≥ 200`, `G,B ≤ 85`) or cyan (`R ≤ 85`, `G,B ≥ 165`) are recolored only if no fully opaque pixel within two pixels confirms that same saturated color. In that case the nearest fully opaque pixel supplies RGB; alpha is unchanged. Fully transparent RGB is extended from the nearest visible pixel to prevent hidden matte colors bleeding during resampling; alpha remains zero. No color key is applied to opaque pixels, and all other semi-transparent RGB values are retained. This preserves real red objects/clothing and white highlights while addressing the known fringe colors. Original source pixels and prompts are never modified.

The map uses source group names and keys, with resources lowercased and `interface` exposed as `ui`; paths stay relative to the game asset root. The source IDs and original keys are also retained in the provenance manifest. Artwork is decorative and does not encode labels, values, statuses, ownership, or gameplay state.

The supplemental atlases and prompts are preserved under `artwork/diorama/supplemental/`, with a separate `build-manifest.json` containing their crop rectangles and hashes. The replacement H01–H03 panorama sources use their full 2172×724 bounds and have their own preserved prompt files and manifest hash. The later H04 combat-clearing panorama is separately preserved with its original manifest and prompt and exports at 640×213 to `scenes.combat-clearing`. The earlier wide atlas and prompt remain archived as discarded sources. They add `materials` and `navigation` maps; the resource-workstation, building-workshop, and equipment-armory panoramas supply the `scenes.colony`, `scenes.building-workshop`, and `scenes.equipment-armory` URLs. Navigation glyphs trim to visible alpha and are capped at 100 px. The replacement panorama files are additional assets; their original `S01`–`S03` image files remain packaged.

Before trimming, alpha bounds at threshold 8 occupied 84.0% of B02 Barn (396×286 of 400×337), 72.0% of B18 Trap (398×294 of 418×389), 66.5% of E12 Shield (407×428 of 512×512), and 70.5% of R01 Food (376×337 of 418×430). The exporter now applies that shared alpha-bound trim to every building, equipment, and resource rendition; the two-pixel pad preserves the outer contour, including the Trap's detached shoulder components.

## Map tiles for special map locations (V-165)

`node tools/build-map-specials.cjs` composes the tiles `MX01`-`MX11` for the special map locations (Hell, Wall, Block, Doom, Prison, Bionic, Star, Darkness, Melting, Frozen, Prismatic) from packaged Diorama renditions only: an object on a terrain slab (`T02`/`T03`), a scene cropped into a rounded wood frame (`S09`, `S10`) or a tinted biome tile (`M05`). No new artwork is painted, so the tiles carry the same provenance and rights as their inputs. Composition runs in headless Chromium (canvas, WebP quality 0.92) because the project's sharp-based exporter is not needed for pure re-composition; the tiles are 444 x 444 px like `M01`-`M08`. The tool writes `mobile-diorama-map-specials.js`, which adds the tiles and the aliases Plentiful -> `M06-Gardens` (Gardens maps carry the location `Plentiful`) and Atlantis -> `M04-Sea` to `DioramaAssets.biomes`, and `artwork/diorama/map-specials.json` with the recipe and the SHA-256 of every input and output. `tests/map-specials.test.cjs` checks that every location of `game.mapConfig.locations` (except `All`) has a Diorama tile and a Classic picture or symbol.
