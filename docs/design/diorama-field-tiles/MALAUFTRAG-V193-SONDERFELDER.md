AUFGABE
Male genau EIN quadratisches Bild nach dieser Vorgabe:

Create ONE square image, 2048×2048 px, containing a 4×4 grid of 16 separate game field tiles on a flat plain dark brown background (#2b1a0e), with at least 32 px of empty background between tiles and around the edge. Match the attached tile sheets exactly in style, size, proportion and lighting: hand-painted cozy fantasy diorama, slightly top-down 3/4 view, rounded square ground top with a clearly visible earth edge (about 10 % of the tile height) at the bottom, soft light from the upper left, crisp fine detail, raised ground, small contact shadows, gentle ambient occlusion. No objects on the ground, no outlines, no text, no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no UI frames, no strong bloom. All tiles identical in size and proportion, about 1.12:1 (width:height). Keep every tile readable on a dark screen and clearly different from the green meadow tiles of the reference.
Row 1 – MAGMA ground, four variants (tiles 1–4): cooled dark basalt crust with glowing orange-red cracks; the cracks form a different pattern in each tile; the earth edge underneath is dark red-brown and faintly glowing at the cracks. Clearly red-orange at first glance, but not a flat colour.
Row 2 – CORRUPTION ground, four variants (tiles 5–8): meadow ground overgrown by violet slime and small purple crystal growths; the grass shows through in places; each tile a different pattern; earth edge dark violet-brown.
Row 3 – (9) and (10): HEALTHY ground, two variants: lush, rich meadow with bright young green shoots, tiny white flowers and a soft golden shimmer. (11) and (12): the plain cleared stone slab with a recessed check-mark from sheet A, with a thin glowing red-orange crack along its edge (beaten Magma cell), two slightly different versions.
Row 4 – (13) the same check-mark slab with a thin violet crystal vein along its edge (beaten Corruption cell); (14) the same check-mark slab with a few small green shoots at its edge (beaten Healthy cell); (15) broken stone ground with glowing orange cracks (Magma overkill); (16) broken stone ground with violet crystal shards (Corruption overkill).
Keep colours and lighting consistent across all 16 tiles so they sit side by side in one grid with the reference tiles.

- Magma (rot-orange), Corruption (violett) und Healthy (sattes Hellgrün mit Goldschimmer) auf einen Blick unterscheidbar, auch stark verkleinert.
