# Änderungen

## 1.00 – Scruffy Colony 1.0
- German help, ADVISOR and legal information now declare their language for correct pronunciation and text handling.
- Enemy ability information, including Fast and dynamic modifiers, and the Gobbleby food bonus now appear in German when German is selected.
- Achievement notifications use a compact parchment card with readable rewards and the original achievement actions in Diorama mode.
- New colonies build and check their first ten Traps twice as fast, including the tutorial Traps. Finishing the tutorial grants five finished Traps once.
- Short one-time explanations cover breeding, the first troop loss, new Hut housing and Barn food storage.
- Perk Optimization is a one-time research after four completed portal runs. Its unlock stays with the save across portals and exports.
- The perk planner has its own opaque parchment workbench, closes cleanly when navigating, and shows a single review action for a new run.
- German gathering research names are clearer, and the portal preparation entry no longer wastes a line on its arrow.
- Time Warp, its help and offline summaries now appear in German. Time Warp and the complete Bone Trader use matching parchment in Diorama mode.
- Concrete bone-package links open the matching Google Play purchase directly. Purchase information remains accessible and is grouped below all packages.
- Help now shows unlocked Advisor chapters directly, followed by the game guide. Help, Heirlooms and Achievements use the matching parchment design in Diorama mode.
- The Helium and Radon per-hour information now shows its title and full explanation in German when German is selected.
- The portal only offers Challenge selection for a new run; current-run Challenge information and actions remain accessible while managing Perks.
- Research now shows all available entries in one continuous scrolling list, without previous/next pages.
- Seasonal Gourdle loot messages now appear in German when German is selected, including empty finds and Sugar Rush.
- A colony diorama and wooden title sign welcome you while the game loads, with a German or English loading hint and no added waiting time.
- Scruffy Colony starts its release history with version 1.0: an offline colony game with Diorama and Classic graphics, German and English, and three independent saves.
- The startup screen stays covered until the selected language, graphics and save are ready. The Map Workshop, map presets and map summaries are translated into German.
- Purchases and their receipts are stored together so an interrupted confirmation cannot add the same bones twice. Deleting a save remains effective after an interrupted restart.
- The guided start still speeds up gathering food and wood, and pauses safely when the app or a protected save operation is suspended.
- Hidden inventory prices do less background work. Unused run statistics and a repeated failed load attempt have been removed.
